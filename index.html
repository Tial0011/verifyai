Verifying AI recommendations in clinical decision-making

VERIFY-AI is a research study that looks at how clinicians and trainees work with AI-generated recommendations when making clinical decisions. This page explains what the study involves before you decide to take part.

About the study

AI tools are increasingly offered alongside clinical decision-making, but it is not yet well understood how clinicians decide whether to trust, verify, or override an AI recommendation. VERIFY-AI studies this process directly, using realistic clinical cases.

What to expect

The study takes place in a single session and follows the same general structure for every participant.

  1. 01

    Background information

    You'll be asked a short set of background questions before the clinical portion begins.

  2. 02

    Review clinical cases

    You'll be presented with a series of clinical cases to review.

  3. 03

    Make an initial decision

    For each case, you'll record your initial clinical decision.

  4. 04

    Review and verify

    Depending on how the study is set up for you, you may review an AI-generated recommendation and work through a verification step.

  5. 05

    Final decision

    You'll record your final decision for each case before moving on.

The exact number of cases, timing, and study arm are not shown here and will be presented as you proceed through the study.
